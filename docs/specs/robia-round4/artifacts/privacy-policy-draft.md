# Robia — Privacy policy (DRAFT for Manu to review and publish)

_Last updated: <date of publication>_

Robia is a personal wardrobe app made by Gusanito Labs ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What Robia stores
- **Photos and garment details** you add (photo, cut-out, name, notes, tags, collections, colours, fit, location).
  They are stored **only on your phone**.
- **Optional Google Drive backup.** If you connect Google Drive, Robia stores a copy of your wardrobe in the private
  app-data folder of **your own** Google Drive (`drive.appdata` scope). Only Robia can read that folder; we never
  receive it. Robia reads your account e-mail only to show which account is connected.
- **Smart features.** Robia can download on-device AI models (from Hugging Face and GitHub). The download only
  requests the model files; **no photo or personal data is sent**. All analysis runs on your phone.

## What we do not do
No account with us, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no sale or sharing of personal data.

## Third parties
Google (Drive API, sign-in, Google Play services / ML Kit on-device components) under Google's privacy policy;
Hugging Face and GitHub serve model files under their own privacy policies.

## Security and deletion
All network traffic uses HTTPS. Uninstalling Robia deletes the data on the phone. To delete the Drive backup, use
Robia › Settings › Google Drive (disconnect and delete backup) or remove Robia's app data in Google Drive settings
(Drive › Settings › Manage apps › Robia › Delete hidden app data).

## Children
Robia is not directed to children under 13.
