Garment review: round 2

Manu chose direction A “Checklist” with four corrections: keep the colour review as it is (only the hint changes), no garment names, 1..N suggested tags per garment, and an interactive review where Accept / Reject are explicit and reviewed garments leave the list. Round 1 stays in ../review-redesign/.

Recommendation: Finalist 1 · Pick, then decide.

The unit of work is the garment, and each garment gets exactly one explicit decision: Reject or Accept N. Every decision makes the list shorter, so “37 left” always counts what remains. Multi-tag garments stay one tap for all or none; picking is tapping the chips you don’t want, and the button counts what will be added. It has half the controls of Tag by tag on the restore-defaults case (7 vs 12) and is cheaper to build.

Shared by both finalists: photo + “category · colour” identity (no name), new-value-first chips with a quiet “Now:” / “instead of” line, pinned “N left” header, Undo snackbar for the last action, a collapsed Reviewed section to recover older decisions, “Accept all remaining” with confirmation, the calm completion screen, and the colour review untouched except for its new hint.

Tokens. Light = Earth & Steel from Color.kt / docs/ux/earth_steel/DESIGN.md (espresso #332421 primary, champagne #f1dcc9 secondary container, terracotta accents). Robia ships light only; the dark phones use round 1’s derived dark proposal of the same palette (imported from ../review-redesign/build.py, unchanged). Fonts render with the fallback stack (Inter/Roboto not installed on the build machine; it is wider, so what fits here fits on device).

4 · Motion

  1. Accept / Reject / Keep as is / last row decided: 0–150 ms the card content cross-fades to a one-line outcome (“Adds Business” / “Kept as it was”); 150–400 ms height → 0 + fade, cards below slide up. FastOutSlowIn, no overshoot, no sparkle. One light haptic tick.
  2. Reduced motion (system “Remove animations”, animator duration scale 0): no morph, no slide; 100 ms fade-out, list settles at once. Focus moves to the next card; TalkBack: “Accepted. 36 left.”
  3. Undo: reverse of 1 (card re-expands at its old index), counter back. Snackbar lives 4 s, one at a time (a new decision replaces it).
  4. Accept all remaining: confirmation dialog → all pending cards fade together (no stagger) → completion screen with an Undo snackbar.
  5. Header: “N left” changes with a 150 ms number cross-fade; the bar animates its width (300 ms, as today).

5 · Recovery: Undo + Reviewed section

Undo only for the last action (snackbar) plus a collapsed “Reviewed · N” section at the end of the list. Undo alone fails at 100+ garments: mistakes are noticed after the snackbar is gone, and the only exit would be discarding the whole review. The section is collapsed and below the pending list, so it never competes with what is left; each row says the outcome first and has “Review again”. After the commit, “Edit changes” on the completion screen reopens the same list.

7 · Open questions for Manu

  1. Which finalist? (a) Pick, then decide — one Reject / Accept N per garment (recommended); (b) Tag by tag — ✕ / ✓ on every suggestion.
  2. Leaving halfway through 100+ garments. (a) As today: Back → “Discard changes?” throws away every decision and the tag change; (b) every garment that leaves the list is saved at once, Back keeps them and the rest stay unchanged (recommended: no lost work); (c) like (b) plus “Continue review” in Manage tags for the rest.
  3. Several groups in one review. Today restore defaults works one group at a time, so one review shows one group. (a) Keep it that way (the cards just drop the group labels); (b) add “Restore all default tags”, reviewing Category + Occasions together as in these mockups; (c) merge reviews started back to back into one list.
