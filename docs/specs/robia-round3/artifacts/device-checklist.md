## 3. Round 3e review: end-to-end scenario

**Changed by Round 3f (after FB3-NOSNACK, FB3-COMPLETE, FB3-CHIP-PICK and FB3-SPARK-HINT):** steps 2, 3 and 6 no longer show a snackbar after a single Apply or Reject (use "N reviewed ▾" › "Review again" to take a decision back; the "N garments updated" message and its Undo in step 4 stay); step 5 and 7 show the new completion arrival; there is no skip-hint line at the top of the review; a long press on a green chip offers another tag.

Setup: use a wardrobe with at least 10 garments that have categories and occasions.
